Senior Researcher at Dolby Laboratories, Bengaluru

I work on video understanding: helping models find where, how, and when something happens in a video, given a query in text, an image, or another video. I recently joined the Advanced Technology Group at Dolby Laboratories, where I work on multimodal AI and long-form video understanding.

Before this, I did my M.Tech and PhD at IIT Jodhpur in the Vision, Language, and Learning Group (VL2G), where I was fortunate to be advised by Dr. Anand Mishra. My thesis looked at grounding objects, relationships, and actions in videos with limited supervision. Earlier, I studied computer science at the University of Delhi, where I worked on text summarization with Prof. Vasudha Bhatnagar.
